# Shift Buddy

it is a shift control app to count shifts and work hours .

The app with have menu with the option of shifts entry and adjust, work place and rate per hour , it will have to count the salary feom day 1 to 15 and from 16 to the end of each month and give a report on how much would be paid each period and over the month and overall . It will jave the option to import and export to local callender

Q: How many workplaces / pay rates do you need to support?
A: One workplace but rate can change over time

Q: How should shifts be entered?
A: to enter and adjust fixed shifts or enter manually ( for example to create multiple shifts template like morning from 6 to 15 and day from 7 30 to 16 30 and so on but will be adjusted easy )

Q: What should "import/export to local calendar" mean here?
A: Export shifts as an .ics file to import into phone calendar

Q: Is this for just you (single user) or should it support multiple users on the same bot?
A: Just me, single user
